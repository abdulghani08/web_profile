# Positioning and Brand Strategy

## Positioning statement

Muhammad Abdul Ghani is a software developer with experience building and maintaining web and mobile applications, working with backend services, APIs and databases, and supporting systems through operational troubleshooting. His background also includes ERP implementation and technical project coordination.

This is a working narrative grounded in the supplied CV; wording may be refined after the owner verifies project-level details.

## Primary headline — proposed

**Software Developer | Full-stack, Backend, Frontend & Mobile Development**

The headline is a positioning choice, not a claim that every technology is used at an expert level.

## Value proposition

A practical combination of application development and real-world system responsibility: development work alongside database, hosting, troubleshooting, ERP, and stakeholder coordination experience.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **Software development** — web and mobile applications.
2. **Engineering foundations** — backend, APIs, databases, integration, maintenance.
3. **Selected project evidence** — problem, role, implementation, verified result.
4. **Systems context** — ERP, operations, reliability, troubleshooting.
5. **Leadership context** — project planning, coordination, team and client communication.

## Voice and tone

- Clear, specific, direct, and professional.
- Technical but understandable to non-specialists.
- Evidence-led rather than self-congratulatory.
- Prefer concrete contribution statements over broad adjectives.
- Avoid unsupported labels such as “expert,” “senior,” “industry-leading,” or “high-performance.”

## Differentiators to validate

- Sole IT partner responsible for MyCendekia web/Android operations and development.
- Platform reported in the CV as serving 3,300+ registered users and approximately 2,000 daily active users.
- Experience across web, mobile, ERP, databases, hosting, and troubleshooting.
- Project coordination experience involving a seven-person production team, 11 projects, and 21 clients.

These metrics must retain their original context and be confirmed as current/appropriate for public use before publication.

## Calls to action — proposed

Primary: View selected projects.  
Secondary: Contact / discuss an opportunity.  
Optional: View LinkedIn and GitHub, once the exact public URLs are verified.

## Language strategy

English is the proposed default; Indonesian is a complete alternative, not a machine-translated afterthought. Preserve meaning and factual claims across both versions. Final localization routing and language-switch behavior remain pending technical/design decisions.
