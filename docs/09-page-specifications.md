# Page Specifications

These are content and behavior requirements. Wireframes and exact UI remain Phase 4 deliverables.

## Home

**Purpose:** Establish identity and route visitors to evidence.  
**Must contain:** name, primary positioning, concise summary, project CTA, contact CTA, selected projects, compact skills or experience evidence.  
**Must avoid:** oversized unsupported claims, fabricated client logos, unnecessary animations, vague tag clouds.

## About

**Purpose:** Explain professional trajectory and working approach.  
**Must contain:** accurate profile summary, development focus, relevant systems/operations context, professional links where verified.  
**Must avoid:** personal information unrelated to professional goals.

## Experience

**Purpose:** Make roles, dates, responsibilities, and context understandable.  
**Must contain:** organization/role, dates, concise responsibility and evidence statements.  
**Must avoid:** implying employment types or dates not confirmed by the owner.

## Projects index

**Purpose:** Help visitors identify relevant work quickly.  
**Must contain:** curated projects, concise descriptions, role, technologies only where verified, category filters only if useful.  
**Must avoid:** presenting every project as production-ready or equally substantial without evidence.

## Project detail / case study

**Purpose:** Demonstrate reasoning and contribution.  
**Must contain:** context, role, work performed, verified technology, evidence/outcome where available, relevant links/media with permission.  
**Must avoid:** private user data, credentials, client secrets, invented metrics.

## Skills

**Purpose:** Summarize technologies and areas of work.  
**Must contain:** grouped, evidence-backed skills; avoid arbitrary percentage proficiency bars.  
**Must avoid:** claiming expertise based solely on a technology appearing in a project list.

## Contact

**Purpose:** Enable professional contact.  
**Must contain:** verified contact method(s) and professional profile links.  
**Must avoid:** exposing a contact form unless its delivery, privacy, spam handling, and security have been designed and tested.

## Shared requirements

All pages must have clear headings, consistent navigation, correct language, responsive layout, page-specific metadata, keyboard access, and no broken links.
